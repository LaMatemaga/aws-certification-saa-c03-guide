Amazon Polly is a cloud service that converts text into lifelike speech. It allows you to create applications that talk, enabling you to build entirely new categories of speech-enabled products. Polly uses advanced deep learning technologies to synthesize speech that sounds like a human voice.

## Key Features

- **Lifelike voices**: Natural-sounding voices across multiple languages and regions.
- **Neural text-to-speech**: Advanced neural network-based speech synthesis for even more natural sound.
- **Standard text-to-speech**: Traditional TTS voices for broad language support.
- **Multiple languages**: Support for over 30 languages and variants.
- **Multiple voices**: Choose from 60+ male and female voices.
- **SSML support**: Speech Synthesis Markup Language for customizing pronunciation.
- **Lexicons**: Create custom pronunciation dictionaries.
- **Speech marks**: Generate metadata for text position, sentences, words, and visemes.
- **Timings**: Control the timing of speech for animation synchronization.
- **Real-time streaming**: Stream audio in real-time for interactive applications.
- **Long-form audio**: Generate long audio files for content like articles or books.
- **MP3, OGG, and PCM formats**: Multiple audio format options.
- **Voice styles**: Different speaking styles like news casting or conversational.
- **Voice engine customization**: Create custom voice engines for domain-specific applications.
- **Newscaster style**: Voices specifically designed for news narration.

## Voice Types

- **Neural voices**: High-quality voices using neural network-based TTS technology.
- **Standard voices**: Voices using traditional concatenative TTS technology.
- **Long-form voices**: Optimized for generating longer audio content.
- **Newscaster voices**: Specifically designed for news-like delivery.
- **Child voices**: Voices that sound like children.
- **Brand voices**: Custom-built voices for specific brands.

## Common Use Cases

- **Content reading**: Convert articles, blogs, or news into audio.
- **E-learning applications**: Create audio for educational content.
- **Accessibility tools**: Make written content accessible to visually impaired users.
- **Interactive voice response (IVR)**: Power telephone systems with natural voices.
- **Public announcements**: Generate announcements for transportation and facilities.
- **Voice assistants**: Create custom voice interfaces.
- **Gaming**: Add voice to game characters and narration.
- **Navigation systems**: Provide spoken directions.
- **Audiobook production**: Convert books to audio format.
- **Podcast generation**: Create audio content from scripts.
- **Language learning**: Pronunciation examples for language students.
- **IoT devices**: Voice output for Internet of Things devices.
- **Presentations**: Add voiceovers to slideshows and presentations.
- **Customer service**: Automated voice responses in customer service applications.

## Pricing

Amazon Polly pricing is based on:

- **Characters processed**: Pay per million characters converted to speech.
- **Voice type**: Standard voices are more economical than Neural voices.
- **Speech marks**: Additional charges for generating speech marks.
- **Long-form audio**: Special pricing for long-form content.

Specific rates vary by region and voice type. A free tier is available for new users that includes a certain number of characters each month.
