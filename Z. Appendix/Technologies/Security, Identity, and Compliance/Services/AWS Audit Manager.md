AWS Audit Manager helps you continuously audit your AWS usage to simplify risk management and compliance with regulations and industry standards. It provides a consistent way to collect and manage evidence relevant to your audits, mapping evidence to the requirements of frameworks you're using.

## Key Features

- **Pre-built frameworks**: Ready-to-use frameworks for common regulations and standards (GDPR, PCI DSS, HIPAA, SOC 2, etc.).
- **Custom frameworks**: Create your own frameworks to meet your specific compliance needs.
- **Automated evidence collection**: Continuously collect evidence from AWS services and resources.
- **Evidence mapping**: Map collected evidence to specific compliance requirements.
- **Assessment management**: Create, manage, and track assessments for your compliance audits.
- **Delegation capabilities**: Assign control sets to specific stakeholders for review.
- **Evidence finder**: Search and filter evidence across multiple assessments.
- **Assessment reports**: Generate assessment reports for internal and external auditors.
- **Integration with AWS Security Hub and AWS Config**: Leverage existing security and compliance data.

## Supported Frameworks

- PCI DSS
- GDPR
- HIPAA
- SOC 2
- NIST 800-53
- CIS AWS Foundations Benchmark
- AWS Best Practices
- Many other industry and regional frameworks

## Common Use Cases

- **Regulatory compliance**: Streamline compliance with regulatory requirements.
- **Security standards adherence**: Monitor compliance with security standards.
- **Audit preparation**: Prepare for internal and external audits.
- **Continuous compliance monitoring**: Maintain ongoing visibility into compliance status.
- **Evidence collection**: Automate the collection of compliance evidence.
- **Risk management**: Support risk management programs with evidence-based assessments.

## Benefits

- **Reduced audit overhead**: Minimize manual evidence collection and reduce audit preparation time.
- **Continuous compliance**: Monitor compliance continuously rather than point-in-time assessments.
- **Centralized evidence management**: Manage all compliance evidence in one place.
- **Standardized approach**: Use a consistent method for compliance across accounts and regions.
- **Improved collaboration**: Enable collaboration between IT, security, and compliance teams.

## Pricing

AWS Audit Manager pricing is based on:

- Number of compliance checks performed
- Number of evidence reports generated
- Data storage for evidence

You only pay for what you use with no upfront commitments.
