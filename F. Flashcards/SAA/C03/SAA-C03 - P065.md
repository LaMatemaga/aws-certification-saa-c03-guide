#Q
*Which service would you recommend for a company that wants to move data into AWS over the internet as quickly as possible?*
1. Amazon S3 Transfer Acceleration
2. AWS Snowball
3. AWS Data Pipeline
4. AWS Direct Connect
?
**Answer:**
[[Amazon S3 Transfer Acceleration]]

**Explanation:**
Amazon S3 Transfer Acceleration enables fast, easy, and secure transfers of files over long distances between your client and your Amazon S3 bucket.
