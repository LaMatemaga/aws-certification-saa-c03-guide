#Q
*The company aims to closely track and comprehensively understand the operational effectiveness of its various resources hosted on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) platform. In order to achieve this objective, they are seeking an AWS service that can offer monitoring capabilities and provide valuable insights into the performance metrics of their AWS resources. Which specific AWS service can fulfill these requirements and facilitate the company's monitoring and analytics needs?*
1. AWS Config
2. AWS X-Ray
3. AWS CloudTrail
4. Amazon CloudWatch
?
**Answer:**
[[Amazon CloudWatch]]

**Explanation:**
Amazon CloudWatch provides monitoring and observability for AWS resources and applications, allowing companies to gain insights into performance and operational data.
