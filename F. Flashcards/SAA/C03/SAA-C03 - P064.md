#Q
*A company wants to design a highly secure and compliant architecture for its web application on AWS. The company requires strong authentication, encryption at rest and in transit, and centralized management of access and permissions. Which AWS service should be used to meet these requirements?*
1. AWS Secrets Manager
2. AWS Certificate Manager
3. AWS IAM
4. AWS WAF
?
**Answer:**
[[AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)]]

**Explanation:**
AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) is a service that enables secure control and management of user access to AWS resources. IAM provides strong authentication, fine-grained access control, and centralized management of access and permissions. It allows the company to enforce encryption at rest and in transit, manage user credentials securely, and ensure compliance with security policies and regulations.
