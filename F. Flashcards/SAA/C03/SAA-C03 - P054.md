#Q
*A multimedia company intends to establish a disaster recovery plan for its AWS-hosted web application, aiming to maintain uninterrupted business operations in the event of a regional outage. To replicate the application environment and data across different AWS regions, which AWS service should the company utilize?*
1. AWS CloudEndure
2. Amazon Route 53
3. AWS Backup
4. AWS CloudFormation
?
**Answer:**
[[AWS CloudEndure]]

**Explanation:**
AWS CloudEndure provides automated disaster recovery by replicating the entire application environment, including the data, across AWS regions. It continuously replicates the data to the target region, ensuring business continuity in the event of a regional failure. AWS CloudEndure can be used to achieve low Recovery Point Objective (RPO) and Recovery Time Objective (RTO) for disaster recovery scenarios.
