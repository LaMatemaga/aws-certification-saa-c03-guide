#Q
*Which of the following AWS services can be used to optimize the costs associated with EC2 instances that have predictable usage patterns? (Multiple options)*
1. Savings Plans
2. Spot Instances
3. Reserved Instances
4. On-Demand Instances
?
**Answer:**
[[Savings Plans]] and [[Reserved Instance]]s

**Explanation:**
Both Reserved Instances and Savings Plans provide a significant discount compared to On-Demand Instance pricing, and are beneficial for predictable usage.
