#Q
*The company is seeking a solution to efficiently store and analyze vast volumes of log data in almost real-time. They require an AWS service that can handle the scale and speed of processing such data. In other words, they need a service that can reliably store large quantities of log data and provide rapid analysis capabilities. Which AWS service would be the most appropriate choice to fulfill these requirements?*
1. Amazon Kinesis
2. AWS Glue
3. Amazon Redshift
4. Amazon DynamoDB 
?
**Answer:**
[[Amazon Kinesis]]

**Explanation:**
Amazon Kinesis is a fully managed service for real-time processing of streaming data at scale, making it suitable for storing and analyzing large amounts of log data in near real-time.
